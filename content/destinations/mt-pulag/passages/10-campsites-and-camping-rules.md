## English
On the Ambangeg Trail, the campsites are Camp 1 in the mossy forest and Camp 2 at the edge of the grassland. Camping on the summit and open fires aren't allowed. In February 2025 the park office closed Camps 1 and 2 for clean-up, and no official reopening notice was found. A November 2025 trip report camped at Camp 2, and a 2026 guide says camping is allowed on weekdays only, as a 2016 DENR notice also said. Ask the park office which camps are open.

## Filipino
Sa Ambangeg Trail, ang mga campsite ay Camp 1 sa mossy forest at Camp 2 sa gilid ng grassland. Bawal mag-camp sa summit at bawal ang open fire. Noong Pebrero 2025, isinara ng park office ang Camp 1 at Camp 2 para linisin, at walang nakitang opisyal na abiso na bukas na ulit. Nag-camp sa Camp 2 ang isang grupo noong Nobyembre 2025, at ayon sa isang 2026 guide, weekdays lang puwedeng mag-camp, gaya ng sabi ng DENR noong 2016. Itanong sa park office kung aling camp ang bukas.
