## English
On Mt. Pulag you may camp only at designated campsites, never on the summit, and open fires are not allowed (use a stove). On the Ambangeg Trail the campsites are Camp 1 in the mossy forest, Camp 2 at the edge of the grassland, and the saddle (Camp 3), which has no water. In February 2025 the park office closed Camps 1 and 2 for clean-up because of litter, saying it would announce their reopening; no official reopening notice was found. A 2026 blog says camping is now allowed on weekdays only, with weekend hikers staying in homestays near the ranger station. Ask the park office which campsites are open when you book, and carry out all your trash.

## Filipino
Sa Mt. Pulag, sa mga itinakdang campsite lang puwedeng mag-camp, hindi kailanman sa summit, at bawal ang open fire (gumamit ng stove). Sa Ambangeg Trail, ang mga campsite ay Camp 1 sa mossy forest, Camp 2 sa gilid ng grassland, at ang saddle (Camp 3), na walang tubig. Noong Pebrero 2025, isinara ng park office ang Camp 1 at Camp 2 para linisin dahil sa basura, at sinabing iaanunsyo nila kung kailan bubuksan ulit; walang nakitang opisyal na abiso ng pagbubukas. Ayon sa isang 2026 blog, weekdays na lang puwedeng mag-camp, at sa mga homestay malapit sa ranger station tumutuloy ang mga hiker tuwing weekend. Itanong sa park office kung aling campsite ang bukas kapag nag-book, at iuwi ang lahat ng basura.
