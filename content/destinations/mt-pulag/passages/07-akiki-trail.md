## English
The Akiki Trail, nicknamed the "Killer Trail", starts at Akiki in Barangay Duacan, Kabayan. Pinoy Mountaineer rates it 7 out of 9: steep pine slopes and 10 to 11 hours of hiking to the summit, usually over 2 to 4 days, with camps and water at Eddet River and Marlboro Country. It closed after tropical cyclone Uwan damaged the Atapuan Bridge in 2025 and reopened on 9 January 2026, the Philippine Information Agency reported; the repaired bridge holds at most three people at a time.

## Filipino
Ang Akiki Trail, na tinatawag na "Killer Trail", ay nagsisimula sa Akiki, Barangay Duacan, Kabayan. 7 sa 9 ito ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer: matarik na pine slopes at 10 hanggang 11 oras na lakaran papuntang summit, karaniwang 2 hanggang 4 na araw, may camp at tubig sa Eddet River at Marlboro Country. Isinara ito matapos masira ng bagyong Uwan ang Atapuan Bridge noong 2025, at binuksan ulit noong 9 Enero 2026 ayon sa Philippine Information Agency; hanggang tatlong tao lang nang sabay ang kaya ng inayos na tulay.
