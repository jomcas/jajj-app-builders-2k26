## English
The Akiki Trail on Mt. Pulag, nicknamed the "Killer Trail", starts at Akiki in Barangay Doacan, Kabayan, next to the park's Welcome Center. Pinoy Mountaineer rates it 7 out of 9: steep pine slopes most of the way, 10 to 11 hours of hiking to the summit, usually over 2 to 4 days. After registering at the Akiki ranger station, hikers reach Eddet River (the usual first camp, with a hanging bridge) in about 2 hours, then Marlboro Country in 3 to 4 more hours, then the grassland at about 2,600 m and the saddle camp, about 15 minutes below the summit. Water is available at Eddet River and just above Marlboro Country. There are no rope sections or river crossings.

## Filipino
Ang Akiki Trail ng Mt. Pulag, na tinatawag na "Killer Trail", ay nagsisimula sa Akiki, Barangay Doacan, Kabayan, katabi ng Welcome Center ng park. 7 sa 9 ito ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer: matarik na pine slopes halos buong daan, 10 hanggang 11 oras na lakaran papuntang summit, karaniwang 2 hanggang 4 na araw. Pagkatapos mag-register sa Akiki ranger station, mararating ang Eddet River (karaniwang unang camp, may hanging bridge) sa mga 2 oras, tapos Marlboro Country sa 3 hanggang 4 na oras pa, tapos ang grassland sa mga 2,600 m at ang saddle camp, mga 15 minuto bago ang summit. May tubig sa Eddet River at sa itaas lang ng Marlboro Country. Walang rope section o tatawiring ilog.
