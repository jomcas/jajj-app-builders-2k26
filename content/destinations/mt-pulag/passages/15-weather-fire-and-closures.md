## English
PAGASA's rainy season runs from June to November. The park board and park office, not the towns, decide closures, and hikes have been suspended in stormy weather even for paid bookings. Check PAGASA's tropical cyclone wind signals before you travel. After tropical cyclone Uwan in 2025, the Akiki Trail stayed closed until January 2026. Grass fires closed the mountain in 2018, 2019 and 2020. The summit grassland has no shelter from thunderstorms; if you hear thunder, open the Lightning Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Hunyo hanggang Nobyembre ang tag-ulan ayon sa PAGASA. Ang park board at park office, hindi ang mga bayan, ang nagpapasya kung isasara ang bundok, at sinususpinde ang hike kapag may bagyo, kahit bayad na ang booking. Tingnan ang tropical cyclone wind signals ng PAGASA bago bumiyahe. Pagkatapos ng bagyong Uwan noong 2025, sarado ang Akiki Trail hanggang Enero 2026. Isinara ang bundok dahil sa sunog sa damuhan noong 2018, 2019 at 2020. Walang masisilungan sa grassland ng summit kapag may kulog; buksan ang Emergency Guide na Lightning.
