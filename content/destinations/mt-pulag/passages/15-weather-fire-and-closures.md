## English
Closures on Mt. Pulag are decided by the park board (PAMB) and the park office, not by the towns. Under DENR policy, hikes have been suspended in stormy weather even for hikers who had already booked and paid. The mountain was also closed after grass fires (2018, 2019 on the Akiki side, and 2020); the 2018 fire was blamed on a camp stove, and DENR pursued charges. Watch PAGASA tropical cyclone wind signals before you travel, and expect cancellations when a storm is near. Never light open fires. The open grassland near the summit has no shelter from thunderstorms; if you hear thunder, open the Lightning Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Ang park board (PAMB) at ang park office ang nagdedesisyon kung isasara ang Mt. Pulag, hindi ang mga bayan. Sa ilalim ng patakaran ng DENR, sinususpinde ang mga hike kapag may bagyo, kahit sa mga naka-book at nakabayad na. Isinara rin ang bundok pagkatapos ng mga sunog sa damuhan (2018, 2019 sa panig ng Akiki, at 2020); sinisi ang isang camp stove sa sunog noong 2018, at nagsampa ng kaso ang DENR. Bantayan ang tropical cyclone wind signals ng PAGASA bago bumiyahe, at asahan ang kanselasyon kapag may paparating na bagyo. Huwag kailanman magsiga. Walang masisilungan sa open grassland malapit sa summit kapag may thunderstorm; kapag may kulog, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Lightning.
