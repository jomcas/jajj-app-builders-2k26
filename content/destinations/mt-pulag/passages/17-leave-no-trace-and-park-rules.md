## English
Mt. Pulag is a protected area, so the Expanded NIPAS Act (RA 11038) applies: littering, dumping waste, taking or disturbing plants and animals, damaging trails, and causing fires are prohibited, with heavy penalties. Litter is the park's biggest problem. Camps 1 and 2 were closed in 2025 for clean-up, and DENR-CAR led a clean-up hike on the Ambangeg Trail that June. Carry out every piece of trash, use the camp toilets, stay on the Trail, and respect the mountain as a sacred place.

## Filipino
Protected area ang Mt. Pulag, kaya umiiral ang Expanded NIPAS Act (RA 11038): bawal magkalat, magtapon ng basura, kumuha o gambalain ang mga halaman at hayop, sirain ang trail, at magdulot ng sunog, at mabigat ang parusa. Basura ang pinakamalaking problema ng park. Isinara ang Camp 1 at Camp 2 noong 2025 para linisin, at nanguna ang DENR-CAR sa isang clean-up hike sa Ambangeg Trail noong Hunyo. Iuwi ang lahat ng basura, gamitin ang toilet sa mga camp, manatili sa Trail, at igalang ang bundok bilang sagradong lugar.
