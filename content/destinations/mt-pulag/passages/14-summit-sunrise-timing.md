## English
Most Ambangeg hikers aim to reach the summit at sunrise. A group camping at Camp 2 in November 2025 left at 4:30 AM and reached the top at 6:00 AM. A 2026 guide describes leaving the ranger station around 2:00 AM instead. In 2022 the park office advised against midnight trekking and asked hikers to keep to allowed trekking times, so follow your guide and the orientation. The summit is cold and windy; if someone gets very cold, open the Hypothermia Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Karamihan ng hiker sa Ambangeg ay gustong umabot sa summit pagsikat ng araw. Isang grupong nag-camp sa Camp 2 noong Nobyembre 2025 ang umalis nang 4:30 AM at nakarating sa tuktok nang 6:00 AM. Ayon naman sa isang 2026 guide, umaalis sila sa ranger station bandang 2:00 AM. Noong 2022, pinayuhan ng park office na iwasan ang midnight trekking at sundin ang pinapayagang oras, kaya sundin ang guide at ang orientation. Malamig at mahangin sa summit; kung may sobrang nilalamig, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Hypothermia.
