## English
Most Mt. Pulag hikers on the Ambangeg Trail aim to reach the summit for sunrise. A common plan is to leave the ranger station or campsite around 2:00 AM and arrive around 5:30 to 6:00 AM; from the ranger station the climb takes about 3 to 4 hours. In 2022 the park office reopened the mountain with advice against midnight trekking and asked hikers to follow the allowed trekking times. No written curfew was found, so follow your guide and the orientation. Do not wait on the cold, windy summit for more than about an hour without proper clothing; the park office has warned this can cause hypothermia. If someone gets very cold, open the Hypothermia Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Karamihan ng hiker sa Ambangeg Trail ng Mt. Pulag ay gustong umabot sa summit para sa sunrise. Karaniwang plano ang umalis sa ranger station o campsite bandang 2:00 AM at dumating bandang 5:30 hanggang 6:00 AM; mga 3 hanggang 4 na oras ang akyat mula ranger station. Noong 2022, binuksan ulit ng park office ang bundok na may payong iwasan ang midnight trekking at sundin ang pinapayagang oras ng trekking. Walang nakitang nakasulat na curfew, kaya sundin ang guide at ang orientation. Huwag maghintay nang mahigit isang oras sa malamig at mahangin na summit kung kulang ang damit; nagbabala ang park office na puwede itong magdulot ng hypothermia. Kung may sobrang nilalamig, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Hypothermia.
