## English
Every hiker on Mt. Pulag, on any Trail, must register and attend an orientation at the DENR Visitor Center in Ambangeg, Bokod, where the park office (PAMO) is based. Akiki hikers do this first, then register again at the Akiki ranger station. Recent guides say reservations must be made in advance and walk-ins are turned away; bookings are handled by the PAMO, reportedly through its Facebook page and by phone during office hours. The park has asked for a "fit to climb" medical certificate since a fatal heart attack at Camp 2, and a 2026 blog says it is still required. Book weekends and holidays early. Confirm all of this with the PAMO before the trip.

## Filipino
Lahat ng hiker sa Mt. Pulag, kahit anong Trail, ay kailangang mag-register at dumalo sa orientation sa DENR Visitor Center sa Ambangeg, Bokod, kung saan naroon ang park office (PAMO). Ang mga dadaan sa Akiki ay dito muna, tapos magre-register ulit sa Akiki ranger station. Ayon sa mga bagong guide, kailangan ng advance reservation at hindi tinatanggap ang walk-in; ang PAMO ang humahawak ng booking, sa Facebook page daw nila at sa telepono tuwing office hours. Humihingi ang park ng "fit to climb" na medical certificate mula nang may namatay sa atake sa puso sa Camp 2, at ayon sa isang 2026 blog, required pa rin ito. Mag-book nang maaga kung weekend o holiday. I-confirm lahat ng ito sa PAMO bago ang biyahe.
