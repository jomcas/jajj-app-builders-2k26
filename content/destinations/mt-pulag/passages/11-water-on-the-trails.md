## English
Mt. Pulag has water sources on its Trails, but none at the summit or at the saddle camp (Camp 3). On the Ambangeg Trail there is a source near Camp 2, about 100 m away, and OpenStreetMap shows springs between Camp 1 and Camp 2 and beside Camp 2; a 2026 blog counts 2 or 3 sources along the way. On the Akiki Trail, water is at Eddet River and just above Marlboro Country. On the Tawangan Trail, the last water is a stream in the grassland before the saddle. Carry at least 2 litres from the ranger station. Treat all water before drinking: open the Purifying water Guide. If someone shows signs of dehydration, open the Dehydration Guide.

## Filipino
May mga water source sa mga Trail ng Mt. Pulag, pero wala sa summit at sa saddle camp (Camp 3). Sa Ambangeg Trail, may source malapit sa Camp 2, mga 100 m ang layo, at sa OpenStreetMap may mga bukal sa pagitan ng Camp 1 at Camp 2 at sa tabi ng Camp 2; ayon sa isang 2026 blog, 2 o 3 ang source sa daan. Sa Akiki Trail, may tubig sa Eddet River at sa itaas lang ng Marlboro Country. Sa Tawangan Trail, ang huling tubig ay isang sapa sa grassland bago ang saddle. Magdala ng hindi bababa sa 2 litro mula ranger station. Linisin muna ang lahat ng tubig bago inumin: buksan ang Guide na Purifying water. Kung may senyales ng dehydration, buksan ang Guide na Dehydration.
