## English
On the Ambangeg Trail, OpenStreetMap shows a spring between Camp 1 and Camp 2 and another beside Camp 2, and Pinoy Mountaineer puts a source about 100 m from Camp 2. There's no water above Camp 2 or at the summit. A 2026 guide says to carry at least 2 litres from the ranger station. No official source says how reliable they are. Treat all water before drinking: open the Purifying water Guide. If someone shows signs of dehydration, open the Dehydration Guide.

## Filipino
Ayon sa OpenStreetMap, may bukal sa Ambangeg Trail sa pagitan ng Camp 1 at Camp 2 at sa tabi ng Camp 2; ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer, may source mga 100 m mula Camp 2. Walang tubig lampas sa Camp 2. Magdala ng 2 litro o higit pa mula ranger station, payo ng isang 2026 guide. Walang opisyal na ulat kung maaasahan ang mga ito. Linisin muna ang lahat ng tubig bago inumin: buksan ang Guide na Purifying water. Kung may senyales ng dehydration, buksan ang Guide na Dehydration.
