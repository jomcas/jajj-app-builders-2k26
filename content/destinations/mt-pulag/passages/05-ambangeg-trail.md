## English
The Ambangeg Trail is the easiest and most used Trail on Mt. Pulag (Pinoy Mountaineer rates it 3 out of 9). After the orientation in Ambangeg, Bokod, hikers ride up to the ranger station in Bashoy, Kabayan, where the Trail starts. It climbs through mossy forest to Camp 1, then to Camp 2 at the edge of the grassland (about 2,700 m), then over open grassland past the saddle (Camp 3) to the summit. It is roughly 7 to 8 km one way. Most hikers take 3 to 5 hours from the ranger station to the summit: about 1 hour to Camp 1, 2 more to Camp 2, then 1 to 2 hours to the top. There are no rope sections, only one steep stretch of steps.

## Filipino
Ang Ambangeg Trail ang pinakamadali at pinakaginagamit na Trail sa Mt. Pulag (3 sa 9 ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer). Pagkatapos ng orientation sa Ambangeg, Bokod, sasakay pataas ang mga hiker papuntang ranger station sa Bashoy, Kabayan, kung saan nagsisimula ang Trail. Umaakyat ito sa mossy forest papuntang Camp 1, tapos sa Camp 2 sa gilid ng grassland (mga 2,700 m), tapos sa open grassland, lampas sa saddle (Camp 3), hanggang summit. Mga 7 hanggang 8 km ito one way. Karamihan ay 3 hanggang 5 oras mula ranger station hanggang summit: mga 1 oras papuntang Camp 1, 2 oras pa papuntang Camp 2, tapos 1 hanggang 2 oras paakyat. Walang rope section, isang matarik na bahagi lang na may mga baitang.
