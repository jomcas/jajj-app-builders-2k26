## English
For any emergency on Mt. Pulag, call 911, the national emergency hotline, if you have signal (signal is weak at Camp 2 and better at the summit). Tell your guide and the rangers at once; they know the fastest way down. No official phone number for the Kabayan or Bokod disaster office was found, so none is listed here. The closest hospital to the Ambangeg ranger station is Dennis Molintas District Hospital in Bokod, about 9 km away in a straight line and further by road. Atok District Hospital is about 13 km away, and Benguet General Hospital in La Trinidad and Baguio General Hospital are each about 35 km away. Police stations are in Kabayan and Bokod.

## Filipino
Para sa anumang emergency sa Mt. Pulag, tumawag sa 911, ang pambansang emergency hotline, kung may signal (mahina ang signal sa Camp 2 at mas maayos sa summit). Sabihan agad ang guide at ang mga ranger; alam nila ang pinakamabilis na daan pababa. Walang nakitang opisyal na numero ng disaster office ng Kabayan o Bokod, kaya walang nakalista rito. Ang pinakamalapit na ospital sa Ambangeg ranger station ay ang Dennis Molintas District Hospital sa Bokod, mga 9 km nang tuwiran at mas malayo pa sa kalsada. Mga 13 km ang Atok District Hospital, at mga 35 km bawat isa ang Benguet General Hospital sa La Trinidad at ang Baguio General Hospital. May police station sa Kabayan at sa Bokod.
