## English
For any emergency on Mt. Pulag, call 911, the national emergency hotline, if you have signal; Pinoy Mountaineer says signal is weak at Camp 2 and better at the summit. Tell your guide and the rangers at once. No official number for the Kabayan or Bokod disaster office was found. The nearest hospital to the ranger station is Dennis Molintas District Hospital in Bokod, about 9 km away in a straight line and further by road. Benguet General Hospital in La Trinidad is about 34 km away.

## Filipino
Sa anumang emergency sa Mt. Pulag, tumawag sa 911, ang pambansang emergency hotline, kung may signal; ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer, mahina ang signal sa Camp 2 at mas maayos sa summit. Sabihan agad ang guide at ang mga ranger. Walang nakitang opisyal na numero ng disaster office ng Kabayan o Bokod. Ang pinakamalapit na ospital sa ranger station ay ang Dennis Molintas District Hospital sa Bokod, mga 9 km nang tuwiran at mas malayo sa kalsada. Mga 34 km naman ang Benguet General Hospital sa La Trinidad.
