## English
Mt. Pulag limits how many people can climb. In 2013 DENR said only 120 climbers were allowed at a time. In February 2016, Rappler reported a cap of 500 trekkers a day and at most 20 people per group. DENR-CAR mentioned a daily carrying capacity again in 2022 without giving a number. 2026 travel guides still report 500 a day, and one says groups are now limited to 10. No official current figure was found, so confirm the limits with the park office when you book.

## Filipino
May limitasyon kung ilan ang puwedeng umakyat sa Mt. Pulag. Noong 2013, sinabi ng DENR na 120 climber lang ang puwede nang sabay-sabay. Noong Pebrero 2016, iniulat ng Rappler na 500 trekker bawat araw ang cap at hanggang 20 tao bawat grupo. Binanggit ulit ng DENR-CAR ang daily carrying capacity noong 2022 pero walang bilang. Ayon sa mga 2026 travel guide, 500 pa rin bawat araw, at sabi ng isa, hanggang 10 na lang bawat grupo. Walang nakitang opisyal na bilang ngayon, kaya i-confirm sa park office kapag nag-book.
