## English
Every Mt. Pulag hiker must register and attend a mandatory orientation at the DENR Visitor Center in Ambangeg, Bokod, before going to the ranger station (Benguet Province, 2026). This applies on every Trail. In 2016 the park began marking the hands of trekkers who had booked and attended, and turned back anyone without the mark (Rappler). A November 2025 group registered there at about 7:00 AM. A 2026 guide says the nearby clinic can have 2-hour queues on weekends, so arrive early.

## Filipino
Lahat ng hiker sa Mt. Pulag ay kailangang mag-register at dumalo sa mandatory orientation sa DENR Visitor Center sa Ambangeg, Bokod, bago pumunta sa ranger station (Benguet Province, 2026). Para ito sa lahat ng Trail. Noong 2016, sinimulan ng park na markahan ang kamay ng mga naka-book at dumalo, at pinabalik ang walang marka (Rappler). Isang grupo noong Nobyembre 2025 ang nag-register doon bandang 7:00 AM. Ayon sa isang 2026 guide, puwedeng umabot ng 2 oras ang pila sa kalapit na clinic tuwing weekend, kaya pumunta nang maaga.
