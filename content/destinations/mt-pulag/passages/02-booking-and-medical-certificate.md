## English
You must book a Mt. Pulag climb with the park office in advance. In 2016 DENR turned back trekkers without a reservation (Rappler), and a 2026 guide (LakbayPinas) says walk-ins are still refused. A "fit to climb" medical certificate is required. Trip reports from November 2025 and February 2026 got one on the way for about ₱150; the 2026 guide names a clinic near the DENR Visitor Center. In 2015 the park head said people with heart problems, high blood pressure or asthma should not climb.

## Filipino
Kailangang mag-book nang maaga sa park office bago umakyat sa Mt. Pulag. Noong 2016, pinabalik ng DENR ang mga walang reservation (Rappler), at ayon sa isang 2026 guide (LakbayPinas), hindi pa rin tinatanggap ang walk-in. Required ang "fit to climb" na medical certificate. Sa mga trip report noong Nobyembre 2025 at Pebrero 2026, kumuha sila nito sa daan sa mga ₱150; may clinic daw malapit sa DENR Visitor Center. Noong 2015, sinabi ng park head na hindi dapat umakyat ang may sakit sa puso, high blood o hika.
