## English
Mt. Pulag is cold all year and coldest from December to February. PAGASA had no station there in 2013, so summit temperatures are reports, such as −2 °C in December 2015 (Philstar). Frost forms on the grassland in cold spells; in January 2026 Kabayan's mayor warned hikers not to underestimate the cold and to dress in layers. The park office has warned that waiting over an hour on the summit without proper clothing can cause hypothermia. If someone is shivering hard or confused, open the Hypothermia Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Malamig sa Mt. Pulag buong taon, lalo na mula Disyembre hanggang Pebrero. Walang station ang PAGASA rito noong 2013, kaya ulat lang ang mga temperatura sa summit, gaya ng −2 °C noong Disyembre 2015 (Philstar). Nagkaka-frost sa grassland; noong Enero 2026, nagbabala ang mayor ng Kabayan na huwag maliitin ang lamig at mag-layer ng damit. Ayon sa park office, ang paghihintay nang mahigit isang oras sa summit nang kulang ang damit ay puwedeng magdulot ng hypothermia. Kung may nanginginig nang husto o nalilito, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Hypothermia.
