## English
Mt. Pulag is cold all year. Near the summit and at the grassland camps, temperatures can drop to 0 °C or lower, especially from December to February, and frost is common (snow has never been recorded). The summit is windy, and fog and rain are frequent; DENR-CAR staff hiked the Ambangeg Trail in cold, foggy weather in June 2025. The park office has warned that waiting more than an hour on the summit for sunrise without proper clothing can cause hypothermia, and a hiker was rescued with hypothermia in December 2024. Prepare for cold weather on every trip, in any month. If someone is shivering hard or confused, open the Hypothermia Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Malamig sa Mt. Pulag buong taon. Malapit sa summit at sa mga camp sa grassland, puwedeng bumaba sa 0 °C o mas mababa pa ang temperatura, lalo na mula Disyembre hanggang Pebrero, at karaniwan ang frost (wala pang naitalang snow). Mahangin sa summit, at madalas ang fog at ulan; umakyat ang DENR-CAR sa Ambangeg Trail sa malamig at maulap na panahon noong Hunyo 2025. Nagbabala ang park office na ang paghihintay ng sunrise sa summit nang mahigit isang oras nang walang tamang damit ay puwedeng magdulot ng hypothermia, at may hiker na na-rescue dahil sa hypothermia noong Disyembre 2024. Maghanda para sa lamig sa bawat akyat, anumang buwan. Kung may nanginginig nang husto o nalilito, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Hypothermia.
