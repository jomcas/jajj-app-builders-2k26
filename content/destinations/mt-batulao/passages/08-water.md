## English
There is no natural water source on either Mt. Batulao Trail, the Old Trail or the New Trail, and OpenStreetMap shows no water Waypoint. When caretakers or vendors are around, water and buko are sold at some campsites and huts: Camp 1 has caretakers, and a hut before the fork sells buko. An older report (2013) mentions a New Trail campsite with a water station supplied from a river about 30 minutes away. Vendors may be absent on weekdays, so do not count on them. Hikers advise carrying 1.5–3 L of water per person. If you notice signs of dehydration, open the Dehydration Guide. To treat water that did not come sealed, open the Purifying water Guide.

## Filipino
Walang natural na mapagkukunan ng tubig sa alinmang Trail ng Mt. Batulao, Old Trail man o New Trail, at walang water Waypoint sa OpenStreetMap. Kapag may caretaker o tindero, may nabibiling tubig at buko sa ilang campsite at kubo: may caretaker sa Camp 1, at may kubo bago ang sangandaan na nagbebenta ng buko. Ayon sa isang lumang ulat (2013), may campsite sa New Trail na may water station na kinukuhanan mula sa ilog na mga 30 minuto ang layo. Baka walang tindero kapag weekdays, kaya huwag umasa sa kanila. Payo ng mga hiker: magdala ng 1.5–3 L na tubig bawat tao. Kung may senyales ng dehydration, buksan ang Dehydration Guide. Para linisin ang tubig na hindi selyado, buksan ang Purifying water Guide.
