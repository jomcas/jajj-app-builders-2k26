## English
Most of Mt. Batulao's Old Trail and New Trail crosses open grassland with almost no shade, so heat, strong sun and dehydration are the main dangers on a clear day. Hikers report that the heat builds after about 10 AM, which is why early starts are common. There is no natural water on either Trail. PAGASA publishes a heat index with four bands: Caution (27–32 °C), Extreme Caution (33–41 °C), Danger (42–51 °C) and Extreme Danger (52 °C and above). If anyone in your group feels unwell from the heat, open the Heat exhaustion and heatstroke Guide now. If you are running low on water or feel very thirsty, open the Dehydration Guide. In an emergency, call 911.

## Filipino
Halos buong Old Trail at New Trail ng Mt. Batulao ay dumadaan sa bukas na damuhan na halos walang lilim, kaya init, matinding araw at dehydration ang pangunahing panganib kapag maaraw. Ayon sa mga hiker, lumalakas ang init pagkalampas ng mga 10 AM, kaya karaniwang maaga nagsisimula ang mga hiker. Walang natural na tubig sa alinmang Trail. May heat index ang PAGASA na may apat na antas: Caution (27–32 °C), Extreme Caution (33–41 °C), Danger (42–51 °C) at Extreme Danger (52 °C pataas). Kung may kasama kang masama ang pakiramdam dahil sa init, buksan agad ang Heat exhaustion and heatstroke Guide. Kung paubos na ang tubig mo o uhaw na uhaw ka, buksan ang Dehydration Guide. Kapag emergency, tumawag sa 911.
