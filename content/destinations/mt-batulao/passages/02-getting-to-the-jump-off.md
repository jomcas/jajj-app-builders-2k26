## English
The jump-off for Mt. Batulao's Old Trail and New Trail is off the Tagaytay–Nasugbu Road, near KC Hillcrest Golf Club (formerly Evercrest), around Km 72. Hikers report taking a bus bound for Nasugbu from Buendia or PITX, getting off on the highway, then riding a tricycle to the jump-off. OpenStreetMap places the "Batulao Jumpoff" at 14.05548 N, 120.82057 E, near Kaylaway II Elementary School, with the village of Kaylaway (also spelled Calayway) just north; no official source confirms the barangay. OpenStreetMap also shows a "Batulao Campers Parking" area. Hikers in 2022 and 2026 reported parking at ₱100 per car and ₱25 per motorcycle; confirm on arrival. A separate jump-off on the Tuy side is also mapped, but the Batulao Tuy Trail from there is recorded as hard to follow.

## Filipino
Ang jump-off ng Old Trail at New Trail ng Mt. Batulao ay nasa gilid ng Tagaytay–Nasugbu Road, malapit sa KC Hillcrest Golf Club (dating Evercrest), mga Km 72. Ayon sa mga hiker, sumakay ng bus na papuntang Nasugbu mula Buendia o PITX, bumaba sa highway, at mag-tricycle papunta sa jump-off. Sa OpenStreetMap, ang "Batulao Jumpoff" ay nasa 14.05548 N, 120.82057 E, malapit sa Kaylaway II Elementary School, at nasa hilaga lang nito ang baryo ng Kaylaway (Calayway din ang baybay); walang opisyal na source na nagkukumpirma ng barangay. May "Batulao Campers Parking" din sa OpenStreetMap. Ayon sa mga hiker noong 2022 at 2026, ₱100 ang parking ng kotse at ₱25 ang motorsiklo; i-confirm pagdating. May hiwalay ding jump-off sa Tuy side, pero nakatala sa mapa na mahirap sundan ang Batulao Tuy Trail mula roon.
