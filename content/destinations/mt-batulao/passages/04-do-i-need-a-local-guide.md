## English
Whether a local guide is required on Mt. Batulao has been unclear for years, and no official ordinance was found. In November 2015, the officer in charge of Nasugbu Tourism said local guides were not required, yet staff at the jump-off still made hikers take one. In 2022, one hiker report said a local guide was required, and another said it was optional but hired one anyway. A 2026 report says a local guide is mandatory for all hikers. In practice, expect to be asked to hire a local guide at the registration area for both the Old Trail and the New Trail. Reported fees: ₱500 per group of five (2022), ₱800 per group of five for a traverse, and ₱1,200 for an overnight Hike. Confirm at registration.

## Filipino
Matagal nang hindi malinaw kung required ang local guide sa Mt. Batulao, at walang nakitang opisyal na ordinansa. Noong November 2015, sinabi ng officer in charge ng Nasugbu Tourism na hindi required ang local guide, pero pinakuha pa rin ng staff sa jump-off ang mga hiker. Noong 2022, sabi ng isang ulat ng hiker ay required ang local guide, at sabi naman ng isa ay optional pero kumuha pa rin sila. Ayon sa isang ulat noong 2026, mandatory ang local guide para sa lahat ng hiker. Sa praktika, asahang hihingan ka ng local guide sa registration area para sa Old Trail at New Trail. Mga naiulat na bayad: ₱500 bawat grupo ng lima (2022), ₱800 bawat grupo ng lima para sa traverse, at ₱1,200 para sa overnight na Hike. I-confirm sa registration.
