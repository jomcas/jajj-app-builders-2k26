## English
Most of Mt. Batulao's Old Trail and New Trail is open grassland, so hikers advise starting before 5:30–6:00 AM to beat the heat; one report says it gets hot after 10 AM. A 2026 report says the registration booth opens at 5:00 AM, but this is unconfirmed. No official curfew or cut-off time was found. One hiker saw no one on the Trail after 3 PM, which is an observation, not a rule. PAGASA's national seasons are: rainy from June to November, cool and dry from December to February, and hot and dry from March to May. Hikers say the Trails get muddy and slippery in the rainy season, while the dry months are the best but hot. On hot days, keep the Heat exhaustion and heatstroke Guide in mind.

## Filipino
Halos buong Old Trail at New Trail ng Mt. Batulao ay bukas na damuhan, kaya payo ng mga hiker na magsimula bago mag-5:30–6:00 AM para maunahan ang init; ayon sa isang ulat, umiinit na pagkalampas ng 10 AM. May ulat noong 2026 na 5:00 AM nagbubukas ang registration booth, pero hindi pa ito kumpirmado. Walang nakitang opisyal na curfew o cut-off time. May isang hiker na walang nakitang tao sa Trail pagkalampas ng 3 PM, pero obserbasyon lang iyon, hindi patakaran. Ayon sa PAGASA, ang mga panahon sa buong bansa ay: tag-ulan mula June hanggang November, malamig at tuyo mula December hanggang February, at mainit at tuyo mula March hanggang May. Sabi ng mga hiker, maputik at madulas ang mga Trail kapag tag-ulan, at pinakamaganda ang tag-araw pero mainit. Kapag mainit ang araw, tandaan ang Heat exhaustion and heatstroke Guide.
