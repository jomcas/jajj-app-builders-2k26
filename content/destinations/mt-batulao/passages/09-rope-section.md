## English
The steepest part of Mt. Batulao's Old Trail is the climb from Camp 8 to the summit, where a fixed rope is in place. Hikers report it takes about 20 minutes to get through. The ground there has loose soil and rock and gets slippery when wet, and one report notes the Old Trail turns steep after Camp 7. A 2026 report also mentions rope assists near Peaks 7 and 8 on the New Trail, but this is not confirmed. No official body has checked the condition of the rope, so test your grip and footing before trusting your weight to it. If someone slips and gets hurt, call 911 and open the Sprains and fractures Guide or the Bleeding wounds Guide.

## Filipino
Ang pinakamatarik na bahagi ng Old Trail ng Mt. Batulao ay ang akyat mula Camp 8 hanggang summit, kung saan may nakakabit na lubid. Ayon sa mga hiker, mga 20 minuto ang aabutin para malampasan ito. Maluwag ang lupa at bato roon at madulas kapag basa, at ayon sa isang ulat, tumatarik ang Old Trail pagkalampas ng Camp 7. May ulat din noong 2026 na may lubid malapit sa Peak 7 at 8 sa New Trail, pero hindi pa ito kumpirmado. Walang opisyal na nagsuri sa kondisyon ng lubid, kaya subukan muna ang kapit at tapak bago ibigay ang buong bigat mo rito. Kung may madulas at masaktan, tumawag sa 911 at buksan ang Sprains and fractures Guide o ang Bleeding wounds Guide.
