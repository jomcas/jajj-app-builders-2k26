## English
These hospitals near the Mt. Batulao jump-off, where the Old Trail and New Trail start, come from OpenStreetMap. Distances are straight-line, so the distance by road is longer. Metropolitan Medical Center – Nasugbu, on the Tagaytay–Nasugbu Road, is about 2.4 km away. De La Salle University Poblete Memorial Hospital in Alfonso, Cavite, is about 10 km. Ospital ng Tagaytay, a city government hospital mapped as having emergency services, is about 14 km. Apacible Memorial District Hospital, a government hospital in Nasugbu, is about 19 km, and Ospital ng Nasugbu is about 21 km. A police outpost on the Tagaytay–Nasugbu Road is about 3.7 km away. Not every facility is confirmed to have an emergency room, so call 911 for help getting to care.
