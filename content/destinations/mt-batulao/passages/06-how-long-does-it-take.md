## English
Time estimates for Mt. Batulao vary with pace and with the Trail chosen. Pinoy Mountaineer gives 2–4 hours up and 1–2 hours down; Transit Pinas gives 2–3 hours up and 1–2 hours down. Other hiker reports give 4–6 hours in total, and some say the whole Hike up and down can take up to 7 hours. From the fork where the two Trails split (about 35–40 minutes from the jump-off), the summit is roughly 1.5–2 hours away on the Old Trail and 1–1.5 hours on the New Trail. The New Trail to Old Trail traverse mapped in OpenStreetMap measures about 7.2 km, including about 2.3 km of access road, while some blogs say 12 km. Because the figures do not match, plan your day by time rather than distance.

## Filipino
Iba-iba ang tantiya ng oras sa Mt. Batulao depende sa bilis ng hiker at sa Trail na pipiliin. Ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer, 2–4 oras paakyat at 1–2 oras pababa; ayon sa Transit Pinas, 2–3 oras paakyat at 1–2 oras pababa. Sa ibang ulat ng mga hiker, 4–6 oras lahat-lahat, at may nagsasabing aabot ng hanggang 7 oras ang buong Hike, akyat at baba. Mula sa sangandaan kung saan naghihiwalay ang dalawang Trail (mga 35–40 minuto mula jump-off), mga 1.5–2 oras pa papuntang summit sa Old Trail at 1–1.5 oras sa New Trail. Ang traverse na New Trail papuntang Old Trail sa OpenStreetMap ay mga 7.2 km, kasama ang mga 2.3 km na access road, pero 12 km naman ang sabi ng ilang blog. Dahil hindi nagtutugma ang mga bilang, magplano ayon sa oras, hindi sa layo.
