## English
Hikers report mobile signal on most parts of Mt. Batulao's Old Trail and New Trail. A 2013 report says signal is present in most parts, a 2022 report says it is mostly present, and another 2022 report found mobile data along the Trail. No official coverage map was checked, so do not depend on signal. Tahak works without signal once the Mt. Batulao Destination Pack is downloaded: the map, Trails, Waypoints and the Guide Library stay on your phone. If you have signal in an emergency, call 911.

## Filipino
Ayon sa mga hiker, may mobile signal sa karamihan ng bahagi ng Old Trail at New Trail ng Mt. Batulao. Sabi ng isang ulat noong 2013, may signal sa karamihan ng bahagi; sabi ng isang ulat noong 2022, kadalasang may signal; at may isa pang ulat noong 2022 na may mobile data sa Trail. Walang opisyal na coverage map na natingnan, kaya huwag umasa sa signal. Gumagana ang Tahak kahit walang signal basta na-download na ang Destination Pack ng Mt. Batulao: nasa phone mo ang mapa, mga Trail, mga Waypoint at ang Guide Library. Kung may signal ka habang may emergency, tumawag sa 911.
