## English
Mt. Batulao's Old Trail and New Trail cross dry grassland that has burned before: blogs recorded brushfires in March 2013 and April 2016, both in the dry season. After the 2013 fire, the grass grew back and the Trails stayed usable. A 2022 hiker report says building fires on Mt. Batulao is prohibited, so do not make a campfire. No official fire-season closure policy was found, and no current closure notice either. Ask at the registration desk at the jump-off whether any closure is in effect before you start. If you see a brushfire on the Trail, move away from it and call 911.

## Filipino
Dumadaan ang Old Trail at New Trail ng Mt. Batulao sa tuyong damuhan na nasunog na dati: may naitalang sunog sa damuhan sa mga blog noong March 2013 at April 2016, parehong tag-araw. Pagkatapos ng sunog noong 2013, tumubo ulit ang damo at nagamit pa rin ang mga Trail. Ayon sa isang ulat ng hiker noong 2022, bawal magsiga sa Mt. Batulao, kaya huwag gumawa ng campfire. Walang nakitang opisyal na patakaran sa pagsasara tuwing tag-araw, at wala ring kasalukuyang abiso ng pagsasara. Magtanong sa registration desk sa jump-off kung may pagsasara bago magsimula. Kung may makita kang sunog sa damuhan sa Trail, lumayo rito at tumawag sa 911.
