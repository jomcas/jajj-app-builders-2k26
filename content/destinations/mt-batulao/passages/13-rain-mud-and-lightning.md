## English
Rain can arrive suddenly in the afternoon on Mt. Batulao. When wet, the loose soil and rock on both the Old Trail and the New Trail turn slippery, especially on the way down, and the rope section from Camp 8 to the summit on the Old Trail is slippery too. Most of both Trails is open grassland with little cover, and the upper ridge gets strong wind. Check PAGASA warnings before you go: a Thunderstorm Advisory means a storm is expected within about 2 hours, and a Thunderstorm Watch means one is likely within 12 hours. PAGASA rainfall warnings come in three levels: Advisory, Alert and Emergency. If you hear thunder or see lightning, open the Lightning Guide right away. If someone slips and is hurt, call 911 and open the Sprains and fractures Guide.

## Filipino
Pwedeng biglang umulan sa hapon sa Mt. Batulao. Kapag basa, nagiging madulas ang maluwag na lupa at bato sa Old Trail at New Trail, lalo na pababa, at madulas din ang rope section mula Camp 8 hanggang summit sa Old Trail. Halos buong dalawang Trail ay bukas na damuhan na kaunti lang ang masisilungan, at malakas ang hangin sa itaas na bahagi ng tagaytay. Tingnan ang mga babala ng PAGASA bago umalis: ang Thunderstorm Advisory ay nangangahulugang may inaasahang thunderstorm sa loob ng mga 2 oras, at ang Thunderstorm Watch ay nangangahulugang malamang magkaroon nito sa loob ng 12 oras. May tatlong antas ang rainfall warning ng PAGASA: Advisory, Alert at Emergency. Kung may marinig kang kulog o makakita ng kidlat, buksan agad ang Lightning Guide. Kung may madulas at masaktan, tumawag sa 911 at buksan ang Sprains and fractures Guide.
