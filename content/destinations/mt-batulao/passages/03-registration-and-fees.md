## English
On Mt. Batulao, hikers register and pay at the registration area at the jump-off, and sign logbooks at Camp 1 and Camp 8. No official fee schedule was found, and hiker reports differ. A May 2026 report listed ₱140 at the jump-off (barangay eco-fee plus tourism tax), ₱20 at Camp 8, ₱40 at the Old Trail descent junction, ₱30 per person per night for camping, and parking at ₱100 per car or ₱25 per motorcycle. A June 2022 report also listed ₱140 at the jump-off, the same parking rates, and ₱25 for a shower. Another 2022 report listed ₱200 for traverse registration, plus separate collections along the Trail. Reports from 2013 to 2017 described ₱20–30 per camp and five or six informal collection points. Bring small bills and confirm amounts at registration.

## Filipino
Sa Mt. Batulao, nagre-register at nagbabayad ang mga hiker sa registration area sa jump-off, at pumipirma sa logbook sa Camp 1 at Camp 8. Walang nakitang opisyal na listahan ng bayarin, at magkakaiba ang ulat ng mga hiker. Ayon sa isang ulat noong May 2026: ₱140 sa jump-off (barangay eco-fee at tourism tax), ₱20 sa Camp 8, ₱40 sa junction pababa ng Old Trail, ₱30 bawat tao bawat gabi para sa camping, at parking na ₱100 sa kotse o ₱25 sa motorsiklo. Isang ulat noong June 2022: ₱140 din sa jump-off, parehong parking, at ₱25 sa shower. Isa pang ulat noong 2022: ₱200 para sa traverse registration, at may hiwalay pang singilan sa Trail. Ayon sa mga ulat mula 2013 hanggang 2017, ₱20–30 bawat camp at lima o anim na impormal na singilan. Magdala ng barya at maliliit na bill, at i-confirm ang halaga sa registration.
