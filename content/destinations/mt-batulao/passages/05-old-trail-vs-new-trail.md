## English
Mt. Batulao has two main Trails. They share the first stretch from the jump-off and split at a fork about 35–40 minutes in: the Old Trail (east side) goes left and the New Trail (west side) goes right. The Old Trail passes numbered camps and ends with a steep climb from Camp 8 to the summit, helped by a fixed rope. The New Trail runs along a ridge over a series of numbered peaks, with an optional side peak called the "Peak of Deception". From the fork, Pinoy Mountaineer estimates 1.5–2 hours to the summit on the Old Trail and 1–1.5 hours on the New Trail. No source rates one Trail as clearly easier. Many hikers traverse, going up one Trail and down the other; reports disagree on which direction is better.

## Filipino
Dalawa ang pangunahing Trail ng Mt. Batulao. Iisa ang simula nila mula sa jump-off, at naghihiwalay sila sa isang sangandaan mga 35–40 minuto mula roon: pakaliwa ang Old Trail (east side) at pakanan ang New Trail (west side). Dumadaan ang Old Trail sa mga numbered na camp at nagtatapos sa matarik na akyat mula Camp 8 hanggang summit, na may nakakabit na lubid. Ang New Trail naman ay sumusunod sa isang tagaytay at dumadaan sa sunod-sunod na numbered na peak, at may optional na side peak na tinatawag na "Peak of Deception". Mula sa sangandaan, tantiya ng Pinoy Mountaineer ay 1.5–2 oras papuntang summit sa Old Trail at 1–1.5 oras sa New Trail. Walang source na nagsasabing malinaw na mas madali ang isa. Maraming hiker ang nagta-traverse, akyat sa isang Trail at baba sa kabila; hindi pareho ang payo ng mga ulat kung aling direksyon ang mas maganda.
