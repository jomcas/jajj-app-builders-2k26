## English
On the Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, every group registers at the Ampucao Barangay Hall before starting. Hikers sign a manifest with emergency contacts, pay their fees, and get a short orientation. Opening hours differ between sources. In 2022, day hikers could register from 5 AM until noon, and overnight hikers from 9 AM until 2 PM, with reservations through an online form. A 2026 blog says registration opens at 4 AM on weekends and 5 AM on weekdays, that small groups can walk in, and does not mention a cut-off. Register early to finish in daylight, and confirm the current hours with the barangay or the Itogon tourism office.

## Filipino
Sa Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, nagre-register muna ang bawat grupo sa Ampucao Barangay Hall bago magsimula. Pumipirma ang mga hiker sa isang manifest na may emergency contacts, nagbabayad ng fees, at may maikling orientation. Magkakaiba ang oras ayon sa sources. Noong 2022, puwedeng mag-register ang mga day hiker mula 5 AM hanggang tanghali, at ang overnight mula 9 AM hanggang 2 PM, na may reservation sa isang online form. Ayon sa isang 2026 blog, nagbubukas ang registration nang 4 AM tuwing weekend at 5 AM tuwing weekday, puwedeng mag-walk in ang maliliit na grupo, at walang binanggit na cut-off. Mag-register nang maaga para matapos habang maliwanag, at i-confirm ang oras sa barangay o sa Itogon tourism office.
