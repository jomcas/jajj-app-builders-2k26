## English
There is no reliable natural water source on the Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, and none at the campsites. The only water on the way has been sold at the small store at Campsite 2 near the summit, which may be closed or sold out. Bring at least 2 litres per person from Ampucao; the ridges have little shade. If someone shows signs of dehydration, open the Dehydration Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Walang maaasahang natural na water source sa Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, at wala rin sa mga campsite. Ang tanging tubig sa daan ay ibinebenta sa maliit na tindahan sa Campsite 2 malapit sa summit, na puwedeng sarado o ubos na. Magdala ng hindi bababa sa 2 litro bawat tao mula Ampucao; kaunti lang ang lilim sa mga ridge. Kung may senyales ng dehydration, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Dehydration.
