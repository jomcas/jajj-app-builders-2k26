## English
Gungal Rock is the most famous photo spot on the Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, and the most dangerous. It is a slanted rock that ends in a cliff drop. Pinoy Mountaineer warns that a careless photo there could be fatal, and rangers limit each person's time on the rock to a few seconds, especially when there is a queue on weekends. Keep your turn short, stay back from the drop, and follow the guide's and rangers' instructions. If someone falls or is injured, call for help and open the Sprains and fractures or Bleeding wounds Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Ang Gungal Rock ang pinakasikat na photo spot sa Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, at ang pinakadelikado. Isa itong pahilig na bato na nagtatapos sa bangin. Nagbabala ang Pinoy Mountaineer na puwedeng ikamatay ang pabayang pagpapa-picture doon, at nililimitahan ng mga ranger sa ilang segundo lang ang bawat tao sa bato, lalo na kapag mahaba ang pila tuwing weekend. Bilisan ang turn mo, lumayo sa bangin, at sundin ang guide at mga ranger. Kung may nahulog o nasaktan, humingi ng tulong at buksan ang Emergency Guide na Sprains and fractures o Bleeding wounds.
