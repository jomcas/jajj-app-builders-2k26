## English
The Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail starts in Barangay Ampucao, Itogon, Benguet, off the road toward Philex. Hikers register at the Ampucao Barangay Hall and park near Ampucao Elementary School, which was also the registration point in 2020. Parking has cost about ₱100 per vehicle. The usual direction is Ampucao to Sta. Fe, so arrange your ride home from Sta. Fe, the exit barangay, before you start. One blog places the jump-off at the Itogon barangay hall; that is a mistake, as every other source says Ampucao.

## Filipino
Nagsisimula ang Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail sa Barangay Ampucao, Itogon, Benguet, sa gilid ng kalsadang papuntang Philex. Nagre-register ang mga hiker sa Ampucao Barangay Hall at pumaparada malapit sa Ampucao Elementary School, na siya ring registration point noong 2020. Mga ₱100 bawat sasakyan ang parking. Karaniwang direksyon ay Ampucao papuntang Sta. Fe, kaya ayusin na ang sasakyan pauwi mula Sta. Fe, ang exit na barangay, bago magsimula. May isang blog na nagsasabing nasa Itogon barangay hall ang jump-off; mali iyon, dahil Ampucao ang sinasabi ng lahat ng ibang sources.
