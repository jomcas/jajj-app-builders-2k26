## English
The Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail closes often for fires and storms, by order of the Itogon mayor, the Benguet governor or the barangay. In 2026 it closed on 20 April for forest fires and reopened on 1 May; it closed again in late July for Typhoon Kiyapo, reopening days later; and a province-wide suspension of adventure tourism from 5 August, due to a tropical storm and the monsoon, was lifted on 14 September. The Eco-Trail resumed operations on 16 September 2026. Before you travel, check the Itogon municipal website or ask the Itogon tourism office whether the Trail is open, especially in the fire season (February to May) or when a storm is near.

## Filipino
Madalas isara ang Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail dahil sa sunog at bagyo, sa utos ng mayor ng Itogon, ng gobernador ng Benguet o ng barangay. Noong 2026, isinara ito noong 20 Abril dahil sa forest fire at binuksan noong 1 Mayo; isinara ulit noong huling bahagi ng Hulyo dahil sa Bagyong Kiyapo, at binuksan pagkalipas ng ilang araw; at inalis noong 14 Setyembre ang suspensyon ng adventure tourism sa buong probinsya na nagsimula noong 5 Agosto dahil sa isang tropical storm at habagat. Nagbukas ulit ang Eco-Trail noong 16 Setyembre 2026. Bago bumiyahe, tingnan ang website ng munisipyo ng Itogon o itanong sa Itogon tourism office kung bukas ang Trail, lalo na sa fire season (Pebrero hanggang Mayo) o kapag may paparating na bagyo.
