## English
Mobile signal on the Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail is patchy, and hikers report none at Campsite 2 below the summit. Do not count on calling or messaging from every point on the Trail. Download the Mt. Ulap Destination Pack before you leave Baguio or Ampucao: the map, Trail, Waypoints and Guides in Tahak work without signal. Your emergency contacts are recorded on the manifest at the Ampucao Barangay Hall.

## Filipino
Pasulpot-sulpot ang signal sa Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, at ayon sa mga hiker, wala sa Campsite 2 sa ibaba ng summit. Huwag umasang makakatawag o makakapag-text sa bawat bahagi ng Trail. I-download ang Mt. Ulap Destination Pack bago umalis ng Baguio o Ampucao: gumagana nang walang signal ang map, Trail, Waypoints at Guides sa Tahak. Nakatala ang emergency contacts mo sa manifest sa Ampucao Barangay Hall.
