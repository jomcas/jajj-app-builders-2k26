## English
The Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail runs along open ridges with little shade or shelter. Wind can be strong, especially in November, nights at the campsites are cold, and fog can roll in and hide the Trail. No Ulap-specific lightning advice was found, but exposed ridges and peaks are dangerous in thunderstorms (US CDC). Stay with your guide, and get off the open ridge when a storm approaches. If thunder starts, open the Lightning Emergency Guide. If fog hides the Trail and you lose your group, open the Lost on the trail Emergency Guide. If someone gets very cold, open the Hypothermia Emergency Guide.

## Filipino
Dumadaan ang Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail sa mga bukas na ridge na halos walang lilim o masisilungan. Puwedeng malakas ang hangin, lalo na tuwing Nobyembre, malamig sa gabi sa mga campsite, at puwedeng biglang bumaba ang fog at matakpan ang Trail. Walang nakitang payo tungkol sa kidlat na para sa Ulap mismo, pero delikado ang mga bukas na ridge at peak kapag may thunderstorm (US CDC). Manatiling kasama ang guide, at umalis sa bukas na ridge kapag may paparating na bagyo. Kapag kumulog, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Lightning. Kung natakpan ng fog ang Trail at nawalay ka sa grupo, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Lost on the trail. Kung may sobrang nilalamig, buksan ang Emergency Guide na Hypothermia.
