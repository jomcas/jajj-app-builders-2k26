## English
Mt. Ulap is a Destination in Itogon, Benguet, south of Baguio. Its main Trail is the Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, a traverse that starts in Barangay Ampucao and ends in Barangay Sta. Fe, crossing open pine and grass ridges with three main high points: Ambanaw-Paoay, Gungal Rock and the Mt. Ulap summit. The summit is about 1,846 m above sea level, the figure Pinoy Mountaineer calls official; other readings give 1,843 m to 1,856 m. There is no official difficulty rating; Pinoy Mountaineer rates it 3 out of 9, a beginner-friendly day hike. The traverse is roughly 8 to 9.5 km and takes about 4 to 6 hours.

## Filipino
Ang Mt. Ulap ay isang Destination sa Itogon, Benguet, sa timog ng Baguio. Ang pangunahing Trail nito ay ang Mt. Ulap Eco-Trail, isang traverse na nagsisimula sa Barangay Ampucao at nagtatapos sa Barangay Sta. Fe, dumadaan sa mga bukas na ridge na may pine at damo, at may tatlong pangunahing matataas na bahagi: Ambanaw-Paoay, Gungal Rock at ang summit ng Mt. Ulap. Mga 1,846 m above sea level ang summit, ang bilang na tinatawag ng Pinoy Mountaineer na opisyal; may ibang sukat na 1,843 m hanggang 1,856 m. Walang opisyal na difficulty rating; 3 sa 9 ito ayon sa Pinoy Mountaineer, isang day hike na pang-beginner. Mga 8 hanggang 9.5 km ang traverse at mga 4 hanggang 6 na oras.
