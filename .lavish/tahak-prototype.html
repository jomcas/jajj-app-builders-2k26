Emergency Guide
Snakebite
Checked against Philippine Red Cross first-aid material
1
Move away from the snake. Don't try to catch or kill it.
2
Keep the person still and calm. Movement spreads venom faster.
3
Keep the bite below heart level. Remove rings, watches and tight boots near the bite.
4
Do not cut, suck or tie the wound. No ice, no herbal pastes.
5
Mark the swelling edge and the time. Write on the skin with a pen.
6
Get them carried down. Fire the Flare if you need to attract help.
