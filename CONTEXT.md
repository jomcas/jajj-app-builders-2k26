# Trail Kit

An offline-first hiking and camping companion for Filipino mountain trails and campsites. Everything needed on the trail works without signal once a Destination Pack is downloaded.

## Places

**Destination**:
A mountain or campsite that hikers travel to.
_Avoid_: Location, spot, mountain (when meaning the general concept)

**Trail**:
A named route on a Destination, from jump-off to summit or campsite. A Destination has one or more Trails.
_Avoid_: Route, path, track

**Waypoint**:
A named point of interest on a Trail, such as a jump-off, campsite, water source, or summit.
_Avoid_: Marker, pin, POI

## Offline content

**Destination Pack**:
Everything about one Destination bundled for offline use: its map, Trails, Waypoints, reference info for the Assistant, and its Forecast. Downloaded while online.
_Avoid_: Bundle, download, offline data

**Forecast**:
A saved 7-day weather forecast for a Destination or the hiker's last known location, labelled with when it was fetched.
_Avoid_: Weather, weather data

**Guide Library**:
The collection of how-to and emergency Guides that ships with the app and is always available, independent of any Destination Pack.
_Avoid_: Tutorials, manual

**Guide**:
A single step-by-step how-to or emergency procedure, such as treating a snakebite or pitching a tent.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, article

## On the trail

**Hike**:
One trip on a Trail, from the moment the hiker starts it until they end it. A Hike runs in either Solo or Group mode.
_Avoid_: Trip, session, activity

**Solo Hike**:
A Hike undertaken by one person with no connection to other hikers' devices. The default mode.

**Group Hike**:
A Hike in which several hikers' devices are linked so they can share positions and Alerts. The group exists only for that one Hike.
_Avoid_: Team hike, party

**Deviation**:
The state of being off the active Trail beyond the allowed distance for longer than the allowed time.
_Avoid_: Off-route, lost

**Flare**:
A local distress signal made with the phone's light, screen, and sound to attract attention.
_Avoid_: SOS button, beacon

**Alert**:
A message pushed to the other members of a Group Hike, such as a Deviation or a Flare.
_Avoid_: Notification, ping

**Assistant**:
The on-device AI that answers hikers' questions using the active Destination Pack and the Guide Library.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, bot, AI

## Administration

**Admin Portal**:
The team-only web tool for authoring Destinations, Trails, Guides, and reference info, and publishing Destination Packs.
_Avoid_: CMS, dashboard, backoffice
